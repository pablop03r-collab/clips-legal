English

clips-gta6 is a personal tool used by a single person, its owner. It prepares short vertical clips of moments from video game livestreams, always crediting and linking the original streamer, and sends them to the owner's own accounts (TikTok drafts and YouTube Shorts). It has no external users and offers no service to third parties.
